# Wbridge Privacy Policy

Android package: `uz.wbridge.pdt`  
Effective: 5 November 2026

## What Wbridge does

Wbridge (uz.wbridge.pdt) is an independent Android location-link bridge designed to pass shared destination coordinates to compatible taxi applications. It is a utility, not a taxi operator, booking platform, payment service or official client of a taxi provider. It does not book rides or process payments.

## Currently supported taxi application

This version supports WB Taxi only, through the installed Android package uz.wildberries.taxi.client and its destination deep link. Other taxi applications are not currently supported. The general taxi-app description explains the purpose of Wbridge; it does not claim that all taxi apps work or promise future integrations. When you select Wbridge to open or share a supported location, or press Open in WB Taxi, the destination is sent to that supported application.

## Independence and third-party names

Wbridge is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by or operated by WB Taxi, Wildberries, Telegram, Google, OpenStreetMap or any taxi provider. Third-party names identify compatibility or the source of a link only; no official status or partnership is claimed. Third-party trademarks and service names belong to their respective owners.

## Information processed on your device

Wbridge reads only the location link or text you send to it, or coordinates you enter. It extracts the destination latitude and longitude. It checks whether WB Taxi is installed and can open the destination. Diagnostics show your Android version and the most recent action, sanitized input, destination coordinates and launch result. Wbridge does not request or determine your current device location.

## Destination handoff and third parties

At your request, Wbridge sends the selected destination coordinates through an Android intent to the supported taxi application; in this version that application is WB Taxi. The receiving taxi application is independently operated and has its own privacy policy, terms, availability and practices. If you choose Open Google Play, Android opens the supported taxi application listing through a compatible store or browser. Wbridge does not include taxi-provider, Telegram or map-provider SDKs and does not control those applications. Check the destination in the receiving app before ordering a ride. Destination display, service availability, fares and ride fulfilment are controlled by the taxi provider.

## No developer collection or tracking

Wbridge has no server, account, analytics, advertising or tracking SDK. It does not transmit data to the developer and its release build has no Internet permission. The developer does not receive or store your links, coordinates, app-usage history or device identifiers. Android and Google Play may independently process installation, crash or usage information under their own settings and policies.

## Retention and removal

The latest received URL and diagnostics are held only in the running app process. Wbridge does not write them to a database, preferences or diagnostic file and does not include them in its Activity saved state. Use Diagnostics > Clear diagnostics to erase those in-memory values, or force-stop the app to end its process. Android and the sending/receiving applications may retain their own task, intent or location records. Wbridge does not control those records. There is no developer-held account or data to delete.

## Clipboard and diagnostics

Wbridge writes to the clipboard only when you press a Copy button. Copied debugging information includes destination coordinates; copying the incoming URL includes its original contents. Copy only information you intend to disclose. Android and permitted applications may access clipboard content. Clearing Wbridge diagnostics does not clear the Android clipboard or other apps’ copies.

## Permissions and security

Wbridge does not request location, storage, contacts, SMS, accessibility or background-service access. It processes supported destinations locally, redacts unrelated received text and URL details from diagnostics, and targets the WB Taxi package for destination handoff. Android enforces application isolation and intent security. No software can guarantee absolute security.

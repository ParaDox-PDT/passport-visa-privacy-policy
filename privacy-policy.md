**"PASSPORT-VISA" (AVIR Mobile App)**

*Oxirgi yangilangan sana / Last updated: 2026-09-22*

## 🇺🇿 O‘ZBEK TILIDAGI VARIANT

Ushbu Maxfiylik siyosati **"PASSPORT-VISA" (AVIR Mobile App)** (keyingi o‘rinlarda — "Ilova") mobil ilovasidan foydalanuvchilarning shaxsga doir ma’lumotlarini to‘plash, ulardan foydalanish, saqlash, himoya qilish va oshkor qilish tartibini belgilaydi.

Biz foydalanuvchilarimizning shaxsiy ma’lumotlari daxlsizligini qadrlaymiz va O‘zbekiston Respublikasining **"Shaxsga doir ma’lumotlar to‘g‘risida"gi Qonuni (O‘RQ-547-son)** hamda xalqaro axborot xavfsizligi standartlari, shuningdek, **Google Play** va **Apple App Store** qoidalariga qat’iy rioya qilamiz.

---

### 1. Ilova haqida va Xizmatlar doirasi (Hukumat/Davlat xizmatlari haqida bildirishnoma)
Ilova fuqarolarga migratsiya, pasport va viza masalalariga oid arizalarni onlayn rasmiylashtirish, xizmatlar holatini kuzatish, ikki tomonlama tasdiqlash jarayonlarida ishtirok etish va belgilangan davlat boji/to‘lovlarni amalga oshirishda qulaylik yaratish maqsadida ishlab chiqilgan.
> **Eslatma (Disclaimer):** Ilova orqali ko‘rsatiladigan xizmatlar vakolatli davlat organlari va qonuniy me’yoriy hujjatlar asosida amalga oshiriladi. Ilova davlat organlarining rasmiy ma’lumotlar bazalari va qonuniy vakolat berilgan xizmat ko‘rsatuvchi API shlyuzlari bilan integratsiya qilingan holda ish yuritadi.

---

### 2. Biz to‘playdigan ma’lumotlar

Ilovadan to‘liq va xavfsiz foydalanishni ta’minlash maqsadida quyidagi shaxsiy ma’lumotlar to‘planishi mumkin:

1. **Shaxsiy identifikatsiya ma’lumotlari:**
   - Telefon raqami (avtorizatsiya va OTP tasdiqlash uchun);
   - Jismoniy shaxsning shaxsiy identifikatsiya raqami (JSHSHIR / PINFL);
   - Pasport yoki ID-karta seriyasi, raqami va berilgan sanasi;
   - Familiyasi, ismi, otasining ismi va tug‘ilgan sanasi.

2. **Biometrik va tasvir ma’lumotlari:**
   - **Yuz qiyofasi fotosuratlari va Liveness (jonlilik) tekshiruvi:** Foydalanuvchi shaxsini masofadan aniq tasdiqlash (Face ID / Identifikatsiya) va arizalarni soxtalashtirishning oldini olish uchun kamera orqali yuz qiyofasi tekshiriladi;
   - **Qurilma biometriyasi (Touch ID / Face ID / Barmoq izi):** Ilovaga tezkor va xavfsiz kirish uchun faqat qurilma operatsion tizimi darajasida tekshiriladi (biometrik xom-ashyo ma’lumotlari bizning serverlarga uzatilmaydi).

3. **Hujjatlar va fayllar:**
   - Ariza topshirish jarayonida foydalanuvchi tomonidan yuklangan hujjat nusxalari (PDF, JPG, PNG formatidagi guvohnomalar, ruxsatnomalar, arizalar).

4. **To‘lov ma’lumotlari:**
   - Davlat boji va xizmat to‘lovlari uchun to‘lov vositalari (bank kartasi tokenlari, tranzaksiya ID raqami, to‘lov summasi va vaqti). *Diqqat: Bank kartasining to‘liq raqami, amal qilish muddati va CVV/CVC maxfiy kodlari bizning serverlarda saqlanmaydi — to‘lovlar litsenziyalangan xavfsiz to‘lov shlyuzlari orqali amalga oshiriladi.*

5. **Qurilma va texnik ma’lumotlar:**
   - Qurilma modeli, operatsion tizim versiyasi, tarmoq ulanish holati;
   - Xavfsizlik ko‘rsatkichlari (Root / Jailbreak mavjudligi tekshiruvi — ruxsatsiz aralashuvlar va xavfli dasturlardan himoyalanish maqsadida).

---

### 3. Foydalaniladigan ruxsatnomalar (Permissions)

Ilova faqat o‘zining asosiy funksiyalarini bajarish uchun zarur bo‘lgan minimal ruxsatlarni so‘raydi:
- **Kamera (Camera):** Foydalanuvchi shaxsini yuz orqali identifikatsiya qilish (Liveness tekshiruvi) hamda kerakli hujjatlarni to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri suratga olib yuklash uchun.
- **Galereya va Xotira (Photo Library / Storage):** Ariza uchun mavjud hujjatlar va fotosuratlarni tanlash hamda to‘lov kvitansiyalari / arizalarni qurilmaga yuklab olish uchun.
- **Biometriya (Biometric / Face ID):** Foydalanuvchi tizimga har safar parolsiz, tez va himoyalangan tarzda kirishi uchun.
- **Internet (Network Access):** Serverlar va davlat axborot tizimlari bilan shifrlangan ma’lumot almashinuvi uchun.

---

### 4. Ma’lumotlardan foydalanish maqsadlari

To‘plangan ma’lumotlar quyidagi maqsadlarda ishlatiladi:
- Foydalanuvchi arizalarini ro‘yxatdan o‘tkazish va tegishli pasport-viza xizmatlarini ko‘rsatish;
- Shaxsni masofadan aniqlash (autentifikatsiya va identifikatsiya);
- Arizalar bo‘yicha ikki tomonlama tasdiqlashni (masalan, mulk egasi roziligini olish) tashkil etish;
- Xizmatlar uchun to‘lovlarni qabul qilish va tasdiqlash kvitansiyalarini shakllantirish;
- Tizim xavfsizligini ta’minlash, firibgarlik va kiberhujumlarning oldini olish;
- Foydalanuvchiga arizalar holati haqida bildirishnomalar yuborish.

---

### 5. Ma’lumotlarni uchinchi shaxslarga berish

Biz foydalanuvchilarning shaxsiy ma’lumotlarini hech kimga sotmaymiz, ijaraga bermaymiz va reklama tarqatuvchi tashkilotlarga taqdim etmaymiz.

Ma’lumotlar faqat quyidagi hollarda uzatilishi mumkin:
1. **Vakolatli davlat organlariga:** O‘zbekiston Respublikasi qonun hujjatlariga muvofiq, pasport, viza va migratsiya xizmatlarini ko‘rsatuvchi vakolatli organlar (IIV MvaFRBB tizimlari) tizimiga ariza yuborilganda;
2. **Sertifikatlangan to‘lov tizimlariga:** Davlat boji va to‘lovlarni muvaffaqiyatli o‘tkazish doirasida;
3. **Qonuniy talablar asosida:** Sud yoki vakolatli huquq-tartibot organlarining rasmiy talablari bo‘yicha O‘zbekiston qonunchiligida belgilangan tartibda.

---

### 6. Ma’lumotlar xavfsizligi va saqlash muddati

- Barcha tarmoq ma’lumotlari **SSL/TLS (HTTPS)** shifrlangan protokollari orqali uzatiladi.
- Qurilmadagi nozik ma’lumotlar (tokenlar) shifrlangan xotirada (**Encrypted / Secure Storage**) saqlanadi.
- Serverlar xalqaro xavfsizlik talablariga va O‘zbekiston Respublikasi hududida shaxsga doir ma’lumotlar bazalarini saqlash bo‘yicha qonun talablariga javob beradi.
- Ma’lumotlar foydalanuvchi hisobi faol bo‘lgan davrda hamda qonunchilikda arizalarni saqlash bo‘yicha belgilangan muddatlar davomida saqlanadi.

---

- **Hisobni va shaxsiy ma’lumotlarni o‘chirish:** Foydalanuvchi ilova sozlamalari orqali ("Profil" -> "Hisobni o‘chirish") yoki rasmiy qo‘llab-quvvatlash xizmati (`support@avir.uz` yoki ko‘rsatilgan aloqa vositalari) orqali o‘z hisobini va unga bog‘langan barcha shaxsiy ma’lumotlarni o‘chirishni so‘rash huquqiga ega.

## 🌐 ENGLISH VERSION (For Google Play & Apple App Store Review)

This Privacy Policy describes how the **"PASSPORT-VISA" (AVIR Mobile App)** ("we", "our", or "Application") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information obtained from users.

We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our users and ensuring compliance with the **Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data" (No. ZRU-547)**, international privacy practices, and the requirements of the **Google Play Developer Policy** and **Apple App Store Review Guidelines**.

---

### 1. Scope and Government Services Disclaimer
The Application facilitates online submission, tracking, and processing of passport, visa, and migration-related administrative services.
> **Government Services Disclaimer:** The Application integrates with authorized state databases and designated API gateways under legal authority and service agreements. The services provided strictly adhere to the regulatory frameworks governing migration and citizenship documentation.

---

### 2. Data We Collect

To provide our core public services securely, we collect the following categories of information:

1. **Personal Identity Information:**
   - Mobile phone number (used for authentication and one-time SMS verification);
   - Personal Identification Number of Physical Person (PINFL / JSHSHIR);
   - Passport or National ID card series, number, and issuance date;
   - Full legal name, date of birth, and gender.

2. **Biometric & Facial Recognition Data:**
   - **Facial Images and Liveness Detection:** Facial images captured via the device camera are used strictly for remote identity verification (Face ID / Liveness challenge) to prevent fraudulent submissions and identity theft;
   - **Device-level Biometrics (Touch ID / Face ID / Fingerprint):** Used solely on-device to facilitate convenient and secure authentication. Raw biometric templates remain protected within the device secure enclave and are never transmitted to our remote servers.

3. **Documents and File Uploads:**
   - Copies of documents (e.g., PDF, JPEG, PNG format documents, certificates, permits) uploaded by the user to support application processing.

4. **Payment and Transaction Data:**
   - Transaction IDs, timestamp, and payment status for state duties and service fees. Full debit/credit card numbers and CVV/CVC codes are processed by certified, secure payment service providers and are never stored on our servers.

5. **Device and Technical Data:**
   - Device model, operating system version, and network connectivity state;
   - Security integrity signals (e.g., Root / Jailbreak detection) to protect the app environment from tampering and malicious activities.

---

### 3. Device Permissions

The Application requests only the minimal device permissions strictly necessary for its features:
- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`, `NSCameraUsageDescription`):** Required to capture user portrait photos for face liveness identity verification and to scan document copies.
- **Photos / Storage (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`, `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`):** Required to upload required documentation and save receipts or application summaries.
- **Biometric (`android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC`, `NSFaceIDUsageDescription`):** Required for local fast and secure authentication into the app.
- **Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required to communicate securely over encrypted HTTPS connections.

---

### 4. How We Use the Information

We process personal data solely for:
- Registering, verifying, and processing migration, passport, and visa service applications;
- Performing remote identity verification and liveness verification;
- Managing two-party approvals (e.g., host/owner verification for temporary registration);
- Processing payment confirmation for administrative duties;
- Ensuring application integrity, data safety, and preventing unauthorized access;
- Sending transactional notifications regarding application statuses.

---

### 5. Third-Party Sharing and Disclosure

We **do not sell, rent, or trade** personal data with third-party advertisers.

Data is shared only in the following legitimate circumstances:
- **Authorized Government Authorities:** Relevant migration, visa, and public administrative authorities responsible for issuing documents and approving requests;
- **Licensed Payment Processors:** To authenticate and conclude fee payments securely;
- **Legal Compliance:** Where required by enforceable court orders, subpoenas, or applicable legislation.

---

### 6. Security and Data Retention

- All communications between the mobile application and backend services are encrypted via **TLS 1.2/1.3 (HTTPS)**.
- Sensitive local credentials and tokens are secured via OS-level secure storage primitives (**EncryptedSharedPreferences / Keychain / Flutter Secure Storage**).
- Personal data is retained only for the period necessary to deliver the requested services and fulfill mandatory statutory records retention obligations.

---

### 7. User Rights and Account / Data Deletion (App Store & Google Play Compliant)

Users have full control over their personal information:
- The right to inspect and update inaccurate personal information;
- **Account Deletion:** Users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data directly within the app settings (**Profile > Delete Account**) or by emailing our data privacy team at `support@avir.uz`.
- Upon verification, all non-statutory personal data will be permanently purged from active systems within 30 days.

---

### 8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for unsupervised use by children under the age of 16. Applications concerning minors must be executed by their lawful parents or legal guardians.
